How it works

Tap a component to select it. Animated settings play their demo.
Hold it to switch the setting. Do it again to switch it back.
Hidden components collapse, as they do in the real app. Tap a hidden setting's row to see it for a moment without changing it.
Sub-options sit under the setting they depend on and appear only while it is on. The arrow next to its title folds them away.
Search (top bar) finds any setting and takes you to it, even a sub-option that is still locked.
Reset puts the current screen back to Morphe's defaults.
Original shows stock YouTube. Morphe shows your settings. UI design, near the end of the screen menu, switches the editor's look: Original, Morphe or Glass.
